Four corner treatments

  
Six rounded faces meeting at 90° leave a triangular notch at each of the eight
    vertices, and the page shows through it. Soft bevel is the shipped default: a
    sharp core of the same edge behind a lightly rounded shell, so a notch looks onto a
    bevel facet and never onto the page.

  






  
Six backdrops

  
Not one picture among them: clouds are drifting gradients, stars are one element
    carrying a list of shadows. day cycle follows the same clock as the sun
    and the moon.

  






  
Six rings

  
The ring shown belongs to the face on top — green up, the laurel wreath turns.

  






  
Nine finishes

  
None is an image: all of them are gradients and computed noise, so they follow the
    palette and stay sharp at any size.
