# emblems.js

One small mark per face, on a 24x24 grid, plus the six families that say which
mark goes where.

## Monoline, not figures

A 110px cube shows each face at about 55px, foreshortened. A drawn figure
becomes a smudge at that size; a single-weight line reads from 24px to 300px
and, being a path, takes any palette colour for free.

## The families

`elements` is the default (owner 2026-08-07). The moon sits on **amethyst**
and water on **sapphire** — the owner's own reading, and not a break with the
DOMY canon where the Moon is Monday's blue: blue IS water and the moon is what
pulls it, while purple is the night the moon hangs in. `elementsDomy` keeps
the dial's strict letter for anyone who wants it.

`planets` and `virtues` are copied from `Gadgets/Watch Academy/SYMBOLISM.md` —
Jupiter yellow, Mars orange, Venus red, Mercury purple, Moon blue, Saturn
green, with each virtue the cure for the opposite face's vice. They are not
invented here. If that canon changes, this file follows it.

`heraldic` is decorative. `ordinal` marks WHICH step of the cycle a face is,
so it only carries meaning in `continuous` mode.
